Rosetta PLLA

Rosetta PLLA Philosophy

Current PLLA containing products, the particles of PLLA is not homogeneous so some troubles may be created like granuloma on long term or inflammation in site of PLLA implementation. In addition all products contain carboxy methyl cellulose as filling agent. Rosetta Kit formula comes to give aesthetic doctors several advantages and solutions through its formulation and preparation technology.

Rosetta Kit Ingredients

1st vial: 200 mg powder of microspherical PLLA particles. 2nd vial: 5 cc solution containing HA, Niacinamide, Tranexamic Acid, PDRN, Copper tripeptide-1 and other active ingredients.

Kit Preparation and IFU

The application and preparation of the product should be performed according to the official instructions and by qualified medical professionals. The number of sessions depends on the individual case and professional assessment.

Rosetta Results

PLLA is a collagen stimulator. Visible skin improvement may develop progressively over time.

The formulation contains active ingredients intended to support moisturizing, rejuvenation and overall skin appearance.
