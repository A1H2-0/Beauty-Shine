Rosetta PLLA Philosophy

Current PLLA containing products, the particles of PLLA is not homogeneous so some troubles may be created like granuloma on long term or inflammation in site of PLLA implementation, in addition all products contain carboxy methyl cellulose as filling agent ,this material water insoluble and make PLLA preparation process is hard, in addition if accidentally injected inside any blood vessel will block it without any way to solve this problem except surgery. In addition PLLA stimulation capacity for fibrolast to create new collagen need long time from 6 to 8 weeks , so some patients who not know this process may doubt of results, for all those reasons Rosetta Kit formula come to give the aesthetic doctors the following advantages and solutions: 1- Rosetta has used new technology to prepare the PLLA particles in homogenous microspherical shape , sizes from 20 to 50 micrones and so avoid any inflammation may occur or granuloma because macrophages when adher to it not form capsule due to the new microhomogenous spherical shape of the particles and only fibroblast be stimulated to generate new collagen and elastin fibers. 2- Rosetta removed CMC from the formula to avoid the troubles that CMC may make it and focus on the stimulation of collagen effect that PLLA is doing. 3- Rosetta added to the formulation, many active ingredients that work on skin rejuvenation, whitening, moisturizing, mild lifting and anti wrinkles with fast effect to make the patient see fast results till the PLLA effects will emerge on long term
