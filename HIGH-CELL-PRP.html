What is HIGH CELL PRP

HIGH CELL PRP : is a simple and efficient technique. Platelets in your own blood are being concentrated in a clear plasma.
When this
plasma is injected into your tissue, a high amount of growth factors are released to stimulate soft tissue regeneration. HIGH CELL PRP : no need for chemical activation, it uses the patient's own collagen for bioactivation. HIGH CELL PRP :is a revolutionary repair system that uses the body's own growth factors helping the tissue to repair and regenerate itself.
